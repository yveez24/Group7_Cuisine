A Taste of Heritage

About Antique

Antique is a province in Western Visayas known for its natural landscapes, rich traditions, and distinctive local cuisine. Its food reflects the creativity and resourcefulness of local hospitality.

From traditional rice dishes to native delicacies, Antique offers visitors a chance to experience Filipino food culture and local hospitality.

Local Food Guide

Signature Dishes & Delicacies

Local dishes and delicacies from Antique.

Traditional Dish
Porok or Porbida

Native chicken cooked in coconut milk. The alupidan leaves give it a fresh, sour taste.

Local Specialty
Ginat-ang Tambo na may Patuyaw

Bamboo shoots cooked in coconut milk with river shrimp and leafy vegetables. It is a common home-cooked dish in Antique.

Pasalubong
Bandi

A sweet snack made of toasted peanuts held together with muscovado sugar. It comes from Antique's sugarcane tradition.
