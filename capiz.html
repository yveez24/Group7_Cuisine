Capiz: Seafood & Cuisine

About Capiz

Capiz is a province in Western Visayas known for its rich seafood culture and coastal communities.

Roxas City, the capital of Capiz, is known for its fresh seafood, local markets, and delicious Filipino dishes.

Signature Dishes

Diwal

A popular shellfish known for its sweet and delicate taste.

Baked-Talaba

Fresh oysters commonly grilled, steamed, or served with sauce.

Kinilaw na Ugpan

Kinilaw na Ugpan is a traditional Filipino dish made by marinating fresh, cleaned tongue shell meat (Lingula unguis, locally known as ugpan or balay shells) in strong vinegar, ginger, onions, and chili peppers

Binakol na Alimango

Binakol na Alimango is a savory, sweet coconut-infused crab soup from Western Visayas featuring fresh mud crabs cooked with tender young coconut meat, coconut juice, lemongrass, and aromatics.
