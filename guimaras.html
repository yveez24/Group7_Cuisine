About Guimaras

Guimaras is a small island province in Western Visayas, Philippines, located between Panay and Negros and separated from Iloilo City by a 15-minute ferry ride across the Iloilo Strait. It is composed of five municipalities: Jordan, the capital, Buenavista, Nueva Valencia, San Lorenzo, and Sibunag. Though the smallest province in the region, Guimaras has built a big reputation as the Mango Capital of the Philippines. The Guimaras mango is world-famous for its exceptional sweetness, aroma, and smooth, fiber-free flesh. To protect its quality, the province strictly bans the importation of mangoes from other areas, keeping the island free from mango pulp and seed weevils. This allows Guimaras to export its mangoes to countries with strict quarantine standards like the United States and Australia. Aside from mangoes, Guimaras is blessed with natural attractions. Visitors can enjoy white sand beaches like Alubihod Beach, go island hopping to Ave Maria Islet, explore caves, and visit the historic Navalas Church in Buenavista, built in 1880. The Guimaras Wind Farm in San Lorenzo, with 27 turbines, is the largest in the Visayas and symbolizes the province's commitment to clean energy. The Guimarasnons are known for their warmth and hospitality. Every May, the province celebrates the colorful Manggahan Festival as thanksgiving for its bountiful harvest. Peaceful, beautiful, and sweet, Guimaras offers an authentic and relaxing island experience away from the crowded tourist spots.

Signature dishes

Mango Beef Bulalo:
For Beef: 1 kg beef shanks with bone marrow (bulalo cut) 1 kg beef brisket, cubed 3 liters water 1 large onion, quartered 1 tbsp whole peppercorns 1 tbsp rock salt / patis to taste For Mango & Veggies: 1 large semi-ripe Guimaras mango - hiwain, medyo maasim pa (huwag hinog na hinog) 1 ripe Guimaras mango - para sa tamis, pang balance 2 corn on the cob, cut into 3 parts 1 bundle pechay / bok choy 2 medium patatas, quartered 1 small cabbage, quartered 2 tanglad, bugbugin (optional pero mas bango) 3 cloves garlic, crushed 1 thumb ginger, sliced Toppings/Sawsawan: Patis, kalamansi, sili
Kinilaw with Mango
Where fresh fish or seafood is "cooked" without heat through chemical curing in vinegar and citrus, then tossed with sweet or tart diced mangoes. Recipe: 1. Hiwain ang fresh tanigue o bangus. 2. Ibabad sa suka at calamansi ng 10 mins. 3. Haluan ng luya, sibuyas, sili, at hilaw na mangga. 4. Timplahan ng asin. Best partner ng Rice at perfect pulutan. Ingredients: fresh fish, Guimaras green mango, vinegar, calamansi, ginger, chili.
Savory Mango
Is a uses crisp, unripe green mangoes or balanced ripe mangoes combined with salty, spicy, or sour ingredients like fish sauce, chili, or shrimp paste. Recipe: 1. Hiwain ang hilaw na mangga ng maninipis. 2. Haluan ng bagoong, kamatis, at sili. 3. Lagyan ng konting asukal para balance. 4. Palamigin bago kainin. Masarap na appetizer o side dish. Ingredients: green mango, bagoong alamang, tomato, chili, sugar.
Pasalubong Tip: Dried mangoes, mango jam, at mango ketchup from Trappist Monastery. Try sa Manggahan Festival tuwing May!
