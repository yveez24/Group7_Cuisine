About Iloilo

Iloilo

Iloilo is a province in Western Visayas. It is known for its rich history, beautiful heritage buildings, and colorful festivals. Iloilo is also famous for its local food and friendly people. The Dinagyang Festival is one of the famous celebrations in Iloilo.

Signature Dishes

La Paz Batchoy

Taste the heart of Iloilo in every bowl! La Paz Batchoy is a tasty noodle soup with pork, noodles, liver, and crispy garlic.

Chicken Inasal

Smoky, juicy, and full of flavor! Chicken Inasal is a popular grilled chicken dish known for its tasty marinade and smoky aroma.

Pancit Molo

Small dumplings with lots of flavor! Pancit Molo is a famous Iloilo soup filled with delicious dumplings.

Pasalubong Tip

Heading home from Iloilo? Don't forget the goodies! Bring home local favorites like Biscocho, Butterscotch, and Barquillos. They are perfect treats to share with family and friends. Before you leave, make sure to try La Paz Batchoy and Pancit Molo. A trip to Iloilo isn't complete without tasting its delicious Ilonggo flavors!
