About Iloilo

Iloilo

Iloilo is a province in Western Visayas. It is known for its rich history, beautiful heritage buildings, and colorful festivals.

Iloilo is also famous for its local food and friendly people. The Dinagyang Festival is one of the well-known celebrations in Iloilo.

Signature Dishes

La Paz Batchoy

A famous noodle soup from Iloilo made with noodles, meat, broth, and toppings.

Pancit Molo

A popular Iloilo soup made with dumplings and a flavorful broth.

Chicken Inasal

Grilled chicken with a flavorful marinade and a smoky taste.

Pasalubong Tip

When visiting Iloilo, try local food and bring home some Ilonggo delicacies for your family.
